# Impeccable Design Context: Le Manoir d'Ombre (Domaine de Valmorin, 1889)

## Target Audience & Purpose
- **Primary Audience**: Creative directors, frontend architects, design engineers, and recruiters inspecting this project via LinkedIn and developer portfolios.
- **Core Mode**: **Experience** & **Read** (Gothic editorial scrollytelling exhibition combined with tactile archival specimens: an interactive portrait salon and a sealed 19th-century grimoire).

## Brand Personality & Literary Direction
- **Atmosphere**: Sepulchral, literary, aristocratic, unsettling, tactile. Inspired by Charles Baudelaire (*Les Fleurs du Mal*), Guy de Maupassant (*Le Horla*), and Gustave Doré's copperplate engravings.
- **Materiality**: Soot-darkened obsidian paper, aged bone vellum, tarnished bronze 1px rules, coagulated carmine sealing wax, and tallow beeswax candlelight.

## Strict Anti-References (What This Must Never Resemble)
- **Zero SaaS Halloween Clichés**: No neon orange (`#ff6600`) paired with electric purple (`#8b5cf6`), no cartoon pumpkins, bats, or emoji (`🎃`, `👻`, `🕯️`, `✨`).
- **Zero AI Template Habits**: No `backdrop-blur` glassmorphism, no 3-equal-column icon-chip grids, no pill badges or tracked uppercase eyebrows above headings, no `Inter`/`Roboto`/`Space Grotesk` typography, no em-dash prose spam.
